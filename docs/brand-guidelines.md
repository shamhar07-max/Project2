# DigitalBurj | Brand Identity Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0 working reference · **Date:** 20 September 2026  
**Status:** Design specification and production starter kit, not a trademark clearance or an independently verified master-vector reconstruction.

## 1. Brand in one sentence

DigitalBurj connects practical education, technology delivery, business intelligence and evidence-backed professional opportunity in one coordinated ecosystem.

**Core narrative:** Learn → Build → Verify → Progress.  
**Public positioning language:** People. Technology. Opportunity.  
**Optional campaign line:** A higher tomorrow.  
**Do not promise** guaranteed employment, visas, earnings, instant capability, or unverified performance figures.

## 2. What has been selected

The reference wordmark is the **bold, wide, uppercase DIGITALBURJ** shown in `DigitalBurj_Approved_Visual_Reference.png`. Preserve its letter proportions and spacing. The letters of **DIGITAL** and **URJ** are deep navy; the initial **B** of BURJ is the distinctive red architectural letter containing ascending white negative-space strokes. Do not replace the chosen display letterforms with an unrelated typeface when creating final master assets.

**DB icon rule:** The standalone monogram uses one navy **D** and one red **B**, aligned to the **same top and bottom cap-height**, with comparable visual weight. The B contains rising white channels. Do not make the B taller, narrower, or appear as a numeral 3. The two letters must remain individually legible at small sizes.

**What these files are:** The approved visual-reference PNG is preserved as supplied. `DigitalBurj_DB_Icon.svg` is a clean, editable geometric **reconstruction** of the equal-height icon. `DigitalBurj_Wordmark_Editable_Draft.svg` is an **editable layout draft**, not a pixel-faithful recreation of the original custom wordmark. A designer should trace and optically refine the reference artwork into final master outlines before registration, large-format fabrication, or use as the sole production source.

**Usage:** The logo is predominantly navy and white. Red identifies the architectural B; do not scatter red over every UI component. On dark ground, use white letters and retain the red B if contrast passes inspection. These are recommended implementation tokens: the reference image alone does not establish laboratory-measured color values.

## 4. Logo system

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| Approved visual-reference PNG | Fidelity target for the selected uppercase wordmark |
| Primary horizontal lockup | Standard website header, stationery and corporate communications |
| Standalone equal-height DB icon | Favicon, app launcher, avatar, social profile and compact UI |
| Reverse version | Navy backgrounds and dark environments |
| Single-color version | Engraving, embossing, monochrome printing |

### Clear space and minimum sizes

Let **x** equal the full height of the capital letters in the logo. Keep at least **0.35x** empty space around the primary logo and **0.25x** around the standalone icon. Start digital testing at **160 px wide** for the full wordmark and **32 px** for the icon; these are provisional minima and must be retested on the final outlined master. Do not use the icon at a size where the D counter or the B's negative-space channels close up.

### Misuse

Do not stretch, rotate, squeeze, add a circle/box by default, apply glow/3D/bevel/gradient effects, recolor the B arbitrarily, separate the D and B vertically, add a skyscraper, insert circuit traces or robot heads, or use a generic substitute font as a final master. Avoid Dubai skyline or Burj Khalifa as the main visual shorthand for the brand.

## 5. Typography

**Logo typography:** The chosen custom-looking bold uppercase wordmark is its own display asset; retain the letterforms of the preserved visual reference. **Supporting text (proposed):** use a well-spaced neutral sans serif such as Inter or IBM Plex Sans, with system sans fallbacks, for headings and body copy. This recommendation is for surrounding communications only; it does not replace the approved logo letters. Use sentence case for ordinary copy and reserve ALL CAPS for compact labels and the wordmark.

## 6. Brand architecture

Use the same corporate DB icon and wordmark in all divisions; distinguish divisions with a text descriptor rather than invented replacement symbols.

- **DigitalBurj Academy** — technology and professional education, practical assessments and capability evidence.
- **DigitalBurj Studio** — validation-first engineering and product delivery.
- **DigitalBurj Business AI** — measurable operational improvement and bounded automation.
- **DigitalBurj Verified Talent** — evidence-backed capability and employer discovery.
- **DigitalBurj Jobs** — opportunities and recruitment, connected to talent profiles.

For lockups, position `ACADEMY`, `STUDIO`, `BUSINESS AI`, `VERIFIED TALENT` or `JOBS` underneath or alongside the standard wordmark in a consistent secondary size. Do **not** assign a different color to the B for every division: the red B is the corporate recognition device.

## 7. Voice, message and sonic identity

**Voice:** direct, precise, optimistic and evidence-led. Show actual work and real people rather than fictional metrics or sweeping guarantees. Keep technical claims understandable and verify examples before publishing.

**Core messaging sequence:** What problem exists → what capability is built → how it is verified → what the person or organization can do next.

**Proposed sonic signature, not recorded or trademark-cleared:** a restrained three-event pattern: short low note, short mid note, longer resolving upper note (e.g. scale degrees 1–3–5), with a subtle tactile click on the final event. Total target duration 1.5–2.0 seconds. Use warm synthesized plucks, no cinematic boom or generic startup chime. Commission original composition and check distinctiveness and usage rights before release.

## 8. Motion and applications

Animate the same geometric negative-space channels inside the B as a brief upward reveal, then resolve to a **static** wordmark. Keep logo animations brief (~0.8–1.4 seconds) and provide a reduced-motion alternative. Use a clear monochrome icon on small app surfaces if the channels lose detail. For office signage, test the negative spaces under real fabrication tolerances.

## 9. Production and governance

**Source-of-truth order:** approved final outlined vector master → approved production exports → approved PNG visual reference → working reconstructions in this kit. Until the outlined master is commissioned, treat the reference PNG as the visual approval target and the included SVGs as editable starting points. Avoid asserting that this artwork is registered, exclusive, or free of similarity to third-party marks without appropriate searches and review.

**Release checklist:** exact `DIGITALBURJ` spelling; red B not numeral 3; navy/red contrast; D/B equal height in icon; readable at 32 px; clear space respected; all services reuse the same mark; adequate light/dark variants; accessibility contrast tested for UI text; no implied employment or visa guarantee.
